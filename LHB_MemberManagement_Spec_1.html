Module Overview

The Member Management module handles all member profile data — both individual and corporate members — migrated from the Informix si_ind_mast and si_cor_mast tables, plus agreements from si_entitlement.

Source data confirmed. Three Informix tables have been analysed: si_ind_mast (individual members), si_cor_mast (corporate members), and si_entitlement (agreements + nominees). The new schema is designed to consolidate and clean these into a unified member record.

Member Types Found in Source Data

👤

Individual Member (I)

Source: si_ind_mast where i_acct_type = 'I'. Contains personal details, IC/passport, spouse, children, employment info, and joint applicant.

🏢

Corporate Member (C)

Source: si_cor_mast where c_acct_type = 'C'. Contains company name, registration no., incorporation date, business nature, and company address.

📄

Agreement / Entitlement

Source: si_entitlement — links to both member types via e_membership_no. Holds agreement details, nominees (up to 2), RCI info, and account status.

Key Observations from Source Data

Membership No. format decoded: e.g. 02099-KL-A-0222/M/I = Sequential No. – Branch – Unit – Seq / M (member) / I (individual) or C (corporate). This is the primary business key and must be preserved exactly.
3 Product types confirmed via e_cocode + e_enttype:
  • e_cocode = "01" or "15" → LHC (Leisure Holidays Club) — Week-based (e_enttype = "W"). Two company codes with different selling prices and tenures.
  • e_cocode = "02" → CP (ConnectionPoints) — Points-based (e_enttype = "P").
Agreement term years confirmed: 30, 33, or 50 years (e_rtu_years).
Data quality issues found — see Observations tab for full list. Some IC numbers are in old format (pre-MyKad), nominee IC fields are inconsistently populated, and some fields use coded values that need a lookup table.
